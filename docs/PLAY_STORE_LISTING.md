## Full description (4000 char max)

```
LeadPilot Telecaller turns every sales call into a coaching opportunity — automatically.

WHAT IT DOES
After you finish a call, LeadPilot picks up the recording your phone already saved, transcribes it, and scores it against your team's playbook — no manual upload, no separate recorder app to remember.

• Automatic call capture — LeadPilot detects when a call ends and locates the recording your phone's dialer already made, so you don't have to manually record or upload anything.
• AI transcription built for Indian languages — accurate speech-to-text for Hindi, Telugu, and mixed-language calls, with speaker separation so it's clear who said what.
• BANT + execution scoring — every call is scored on Budget, Authority, Need, and Timeline, plus how well you ran the call: opening, discovery, pitch, objection handling, and closing. Every score comes with the exact quote from the call it's based on, so it's never a black box.
• Lead pipeline, built in — track leads from first contact to close, with a simple pipeline you can advance with a tap.
• Performance dashboard — see your calls, connect rate, quality trend, and talk time over any date range, and know exactly where to improve.
• Attendance & shift tracking — clock in, clock out, and let your manager see your day without a status update.
• Coaching feedback — get a clear summary of what went well and what to work on after every call, not just a score.
• Works offline-first — capture and queue call uploads even with a spotty connection; nothing gets lost.

WHO IT'S FOR
Built for telecalling and inside-sales teams who want a coach in every call, not just a call log.

ABOUT PERMISSIONS
LeadPilot Telecaller requests broad file access ("All files access") and call log access. These are used only to locate the call-recording file your phone's own dialer app saves after a call, and to detect when a call has ended. LeadPilot does not use your microphone directly, does not access your photos or other personal files, and does not track your location. Full details: see our Privacy Policy.

Note: because LeadPilot relies on your phone's dialer to record calls, reliability varies by device manufacturer — it works best on Xiaomi/MIUI, Samsung, Oppo/Realme/OnePlus, Vivo, Motorola, and Huawei/Honor devices. Support for other devices is expanding.
```
